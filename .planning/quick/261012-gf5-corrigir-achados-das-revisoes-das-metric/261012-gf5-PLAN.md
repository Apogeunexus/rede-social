## Planner decisions (discretion areas, apply as written)

1. **C-1 merge rule.** Start with one group per band in `ENGAGEMENT_BAND_ORDER`. While there is more than one group and some group is under `PRIVACY_FLOOR`, take the smallest such group (on a tie, the lowest index). Merge it with its only neighbour if it is at either end. Otherwise merge it with the smaller of its two neighbours (on a tie, the previous one). The merged group keeps band order. This matches the CONTEXT example: superfãs 2 sits between novos 22 and engajados 8, so it merges with engajados into "Superfãs e engajados · 10". If the whole base is under the floor, the result is a single group of all six bands. The members KPI already shows that total exactly, so nothing new is exposed.
2. **Collapsible summary guard (C-1, same leak class).** "{percent} em risco" shows only when `at_risk` is a single-band group. If `at_risk` were merged, its exact percent next to the merged row would give the neighbour back by subtraction. With the fixture, at_risk 96 stays alone, so "31% em risco" is unchanged.
3. **C-2 rule.** `isComplementBelowPrivacyFloor(part, whole)` is true when `part >= PRIVACY_FLOOR` and `whole - part < PRIVACY_FLOOR`, including 0. In that case the KPI value is "Todos, exceto menos de 5" and the note is "14 entraram", following the CONTEXT's suggested phrasing. If `part` is itself under the floor, the existing "menos de 5" value and "de {joined} que entraram" note stay. The complement is then a range, not a number.
4. **F-1 scope.** Catalog only: `clientStatus.at_risk`, `platformOwner.clients.columns.atRisk`, and the level-1 bell title `platformOwner.attention.atRisk.title`. The bell says "está em risco" about the same client status, so leaving it would bring the ambiguity back. Fixture ids stay as they are (no unrelated rename). The new pill also shows on levels 2 and 3, where the same status is rendered.
5. **F-3 layout.** The durations table and the stories line stay in the renamed "Vídeos, reels e stories" section. Together they report the period's video, reel and story output, so F-2's section still exists and the stories line stays visible. The six metric cards (4 from the posts section, 2 from the videos section) become ONE `Collapsible`, closed by default, placed right after "Vídeos, reels e stories" and before "Eventos". Each `li` shows the metric label (bold) followed by its `notMeasuredReason` text, with no cards and no "Ainda não medido" word. D-03 now has two renderings. A single unmeasured KPI is still `MissingKpi` (Receita mensal on level 1, Conversão por produto on level 6). The level-5 group is this list. Neither ever shows a number.
6. **F-4 summaries.** Formats: "{format} rende mais: {perPost} por post" (Vídeo, 96). Buyers: the existing computed sentence `tenantOwner.buyers.sentence` moves into the summary, and its paragraph inside the body is removed, so the text is not shown twice. The bar list and footnote stay inside.
7. **F-5.** Add `formatBrlCompact(cents)` to `packages/contracts/src/money.ts` (cents < 100_000 → `formatBrl(cents)`; otherwise compact BRL, ≤1 fraction digit), keeping money's "÷100 in one file" rule. It is used for level 1 "Vendas nas lojas dos clientes" only. Level 4's "R$ 8.940,00" fits on one line in the fcr 390 px screenshot, so it stays as it is. The level-1 grid keeps 6 cards: Clientes ativos, Novos clientes, Membros na base, Armazenamento, Membros engajados, Vendas nas lojas dos clientes. "Receita mensal" (`MissingKpi`) renders right after the grid, full width, outside any grid.
8. **F-6.** Widen `newSinceLastVisit.posts` to `number | NotMeasured`. Without the widening the news card could never be measured, which would make the locked "news gets a slot" priority untestable. `selectForYouCards` drops a card when any value in its data object is NotMeasured. Ana's fixture stays NotMeasured, so it is filtered out. When news is measured, its card shows "{count} publicações novas", and the member panel no longer has a "Ainda não medido" pill.
9. **F-7.** Ana's support reply becomes "Oi, Ana! Pode levar um acompanhante no encontro de hoje." It is about the event card above it and does not touch the VIP access.
